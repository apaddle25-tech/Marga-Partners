The Marga Exchange™

What we owe you, and what we ask.

Version 1.0. Last updated 13 August 2026.

You are giving us commercially sensitive information about your practice. These terms say what we do with it, what we will never do with it, and what you can take back at any time. They are short on purpose, and they are meant to be read once and understood.

Independence

No manufacturer buys access to the Exchange. Not to the panel, not to a seat, not to your data, and not to a question. Marga advises manufacturers, which is exactly why this line is drawn in public. Our advisory clients get our analysis. They never get you.

Your submission is never sold and never licensed. It never leaves Marga as a row that could be traced back to your practice.

We will never put a question to you on behalf of a client who wants your answer attributed to you. Where a question comes from an engagement, the answer enters the aggregate the same way every other answer does.

Transparency

Every financial interest Marga holds is published, and it is updated before a read that touches it goes out. Read the independence and conflicts statement.

How members are chosen for a survey is published. Selection uses what you told us when you joined: what you treat, how your practice is structured, and what you can see from where you sit. Nobody is asked a question their vantage cannot answer honestly, because a guess entering the aggregate looks exactly like an observation.

Every published read states what it rests on, how much of the ground it covers, and where it is thin. A number we cannot defend does not appear.

When these terms change we tell you what changed and why, and we ask you again. Your agreement is recorded against the version you actually read.

Elevating the industry

Manufacturers research this market. Investors model it. Almost nobody gives the intelligence back to the practices it came from, so a practice negotiates against numbers it has never been allowed to see.

The Exchange exists to close that gap. Your benchmark is the immediate return. The aggregate reads are the longer one, and they are what let a practice hold a position with a vendor on evidence rather than on instinct.

What you keep

Your name. Nothing is attributed to you or your practice without your written consent, asked for each time it comes up. Refusing costs you nothing and changes nothing else about your membership.

The right to leave. Delete your submission whenever you want, with no reason required and no conversation to have first. Deletion removes your figures, your contact details and your report.

The right to skip. Every survey question is optional, every time, and no explanation is owed for a blank. A survey you ignore entirely has no consequence.

Your privacy in the output. Nothing identifiable to your practice appears in any published benchmark, read or article. Aggregates are built so that a single practice cannot be reconstructed from them.

What we ask of you

Answer honestly, or skip. A guess that reaches the aggregate looking like an observation is worse than a blank, and it is the one thing that would make this worth less to everybody in it.

Tell us if your practice changes materially. Locations, ownership or scale moving means your profile no longer places you correctly, and the questions we send stop fitting.

That is all we ask.

Membership is capped

We admit the number of practices we can serve properly. A member we cannot survey thoughtfully, brief carefully and come back to is a member we have taken for granted, and the cap exists so that does not happen while the Exchange is young.

The cap moves as our capacity grows. If you apply while it is full you go on the waitlist in the order you arrived, and we write to you when your submission has been reviewed.
